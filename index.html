01 / DIRECTION

From biological data
to general intelligence.

My research direction sits at the intersection of modern AI and the life sciences: learning representations of cells, brains and biological systems; building agents that can operate through scientific workflows; and using biological principles to rethink how intelligent systems are designed.

The north star: AI systems capable of forming hypotheses, using tools, learning from experiments and progressively extending scientific understanding.
